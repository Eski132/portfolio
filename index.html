THE ESki132 INCIDENT

THE GREAT DELEGATION

In the beginning there was a laptop, an unfinished website, one lazy human, and four cats who had not agreed to any of this.

The human looked at the work ahead and performed the oldest engineering maneuver known to civilization: delegation.

ARCHIVE 01 // THE ERA OF THE KEYBOARD

The cats discovered that every key produced a reaction from the human. This made the keyboard the most entertaining object in the room. Miso tested Escape. Fork preferred Backspace. Pixel developed a technique involving both paws and no discernible objective. JPEG slept across the function row. The human called the resulting document corrupted. Management called it collaborative writing.

ARCHIVE 01 // THE TREAT NEGOTIATIONS

A bowl became visible but remained empty for seven unacceptable seconds. Work ceased immediately. Miso established a blockade between the human and the screen. Fork occupied the mouse. Pixel began knocking small objects into increasingly strategic locations. JPEG remained asleep but was counted as participating. The human surrendered. Operations resumed after crunching noises were verified.

ARCHIVE 01 // THE WINDOW CAMPAIGN

A bird appeared outside. Every active project lost priority. Four specialists assembled at the glass and entered a state of absolute concentration previously considered impossible. The bird left. Nobody moved. The possibility of its return became the organization's largest ongoing research program.

ARCHIVE 01 // THE CABLE QUESTION

Fork discovered a cable hanging behind the desk. Nobody knew what it powered. This increased its value enormously. The cable was attacked from three angles, investigated beneath a chair, abandoned, rediscovered, and finally declared structurally suspicious. The human tied it out of reach. This act was entered into the permanent record.

ARCHIVE 01 // THE BOX ECONOMY

A package arrived containing expensive equipment. The equipment was ignored. The cardboard container became contested territory within minutes. Miso occupied it first. Pixel challenged the occupation. Fork attempted entry through a wall rather than the opening. JPEG waited until everyone left and inherited the entire property without conflict.

ARCHIVE 01 // THE THREE A.M. EMERGENCY

At precisely an unreasonable hour, a sound occurred somewhere. Its source was unknown and therefore extremely dangerous. The cats crossed the apartment at speeds never demonstrated during daylight. The human woke to find four silhouettes investigating a wall. Nothing was found. The wall remains under observation.

ARCHIVE 01 // THE CURSOR DOCTRINE

Pixel proved that a cursor could move without being physically caught. This was unacceptable. Weeks of research followed. Paw met glass. Cursor escaped. Paw returned. Cursor escaped again. Pixel concluded that the screen was cheating and has maintained this position ever since.

ARCHIVE 01 // THE WARM MACHINE

JPEG discovered that compiling software increased laptop temperature. Productivity therefore became personally beneficial. For several days the human believed JPEG had developed an interest in programming. In reality JPEG had developed an interest in thermodynamics. The misunderstanding was never corrected.

ARCHIVE 01 // THE EDGE

Fork's fascination with edges became institutional policy. Any object placed within paw distance of an edge entered a probationary period. Pens failed. Coins failed. A USB adapter survived two days before an unexpected audit. The human gradually learned to keep important objects away from the perimeter. Fork considered this successful training.

ARCHIVE 01 // THE SILENT HOUR

For one remarkable hour, every cat slept at once. The human worked uninterrupted. Messages were answered. Code was written. A bug was fixed. The room was peaceful. Then Miso woke, stretched, stepped directly on the keyboard, and opened a window nobody knew existed. Normal operations resumed.

ARCHIVE 01 // THE RED DOT RETURNS

The red dot reappeared without warning. Pixel launched immediately. Miso joined despite pretending not to care. Fork attacked the wall several centimeters away from the target. JPEG watched from a safe distance and contributed nothing. The dot vanished. Victory was claimed unanimously.

ARCHIVE 01 // THE GREAT REFUSAL

The human attempted to move Miso from the chair. Miso became approximately three times heavier. No known physical law explained the increase. A second attempt produced the same result. The human acquired another chair. Miso eventually moved to that one.

ARCHIVE 01 // THE PAPER BAG

A paper bag entered the room and began making noises. This was either prey, shelter, or a sophisticated trap. Pixel entered first. The bag moved. Fork attacked the moving bag. Pixel attacked Fork from inside the bag. Miso supervised. JPEG sat on the bag after everyone became tired.

ARCHIVE 01 // THE DEPLOYMENT

The human announced that the website was ready to deploy. The cats inspected the screen without understanding any of it. Miso blinked slowly. Fork yawned. Pixel noticed the cursor. JPEG was unavailable. These signals were interpreted as approval. The deployment succeeded, permanently damaging the human's confidence in formal review processes.

ARCHIVE 01 // THE SECOND DEPLOYMENT

A tiny mistake was discovered. The human reopened the project. Miso returned to management. Fork returned to testing. Pixel returned to the cursor. JPEG had never left. The organization realized there would always be another deployment. This was when the cats understood they had achieved permanent employment.

ARCHIVE 01 // THE HUMAN'S THEORY

The human maintained that the cats were assistants. Evidence suggested otherwise. Assistants do not own the chair, schedule meals, control access to the keyboard, interrupt meetings, regulate bedtime, inspect every package, and determine when work is allowed to continue. The title remained for tax purposes only.

ARCHIVE 01 // THE FINAL POLICY

After extensive consideration, management produced its clearest rule. The website may change. The code may change. The machine may change. The human may even become less lazy, although forecasts remain pessimistic. But the cats retain veto power over everything, especially anything placed near an edge.
